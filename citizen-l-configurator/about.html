Elevated by radiance

Cheeto Watches blends contemporary femininity with natural light. Every curve, polished edge, and subtle reflection celebrates graceful confidence.

Our founders

Cheeto Watches began as a shared vision between two founders who wanted a calmer, more luminous kind of luxury: honest materials, light-powered movements, and digital experiences that feel as considered as the watches themselves.

Ghania Tanveer

Co-founder & creative director

Ghania leads the soul of the brand: storytelling, visual language, and the emotional rhythm of every collection. She draws inspiration from dawn light on water, pearl-soft palettes, and the quiet confidence of women who wear their values on the wrist.

From art direction on the site to the mood of each campaign, her work keeps Cheeto Watches gentle, refined, and unmistakably human.

Muhammad Haseeb

Co-founder & product & technology lead

Haseeb shapes how the brand meets the world in motion: the configurator, interactive previews, and the systems that make browsing and building a watch feel effortless. He cares about performance, clarity, and small details that reward curiosity without noise.

Together with Ghania, he bridges craft and code so the promise of the watches is reflected in every scroll, tap, and transition across the Cheeto Watches experience.

Craftsmanship

Our watches are assembled with refined precision using sustainably sourced materials and ethical production standards.

Our light-powered calibers harness any light source to run your watch continuously, so the ritual of battery changes never interrupts your story.
